# Savi — art prompts

Prompts for generating the game's art in the Pardhan Gond style. Paste **Block A**
(style) + **Block B** (world) at the top of every request, then one of the
numbered prompts below.

> **Check the jam rules before you use these.** Art from an image model is
> AI-generated content, which is a different thing from art drawn by code, and
> most jams either ban it or require it to be declared. If Cozy Fall 26 doesn't
> allow it, the procedural art stays and this file waits for a jam that does.

---

## BLOCK A — the style (always include)

> Pardhan Gond painting from Madhya Pradesh, in the Jangarh Kalam manner,
> acrylic on canvas.
>
> A bold soot-black outline around every single form. Flat unshaded colour
> inside it — no gradients, no highlights, no rendering, no light source, no
> perspective, no depth of field.
>
> Every surface without exception, including the background, is densely filled
> with repeated hand-drawn micro-motifs: fine rows of dots, comb lines, short
> dashes, fish scales, crescents, seed shapes, water droplets, young paddy
> shoots. The marks **follow the contour of the form they fill** — they run
> lengthwise down an arm or a branch, they curve over an animal's back, they
> close in concentric rings around a head. They are never a straight grid laid
> over a shape. There is no empty space anywhere in the picture.
>
> Bright, high-contrast acrylic colour on a dark ground: vermilion red,
> turmeric yellow, leaf green, teal, indigo blue, jamun purple, magenta-pink,
> saffron orange, bone white. Three or four hues fight inside a single figure
> and change along its length by switching pattern, never by blending.
>
> Figures are flattened and frontal with large almond eyes. Trees, birds, fish
> and animals interlock with the figures and grow out of them. Everything is
> alive and everything is patterned.
>
> Hand-painted folk art. No photorealism, no 3D, no cel shading, no anime, no
> digital airbrush, no watermark, no text.

---

## BLOCK B — the world (always include)

> For a cozy video game called **Savi**. An autumn valley where the fall has
> lasted too long and a permanent winter is coming. At its centre stands a
> colossal leafless banyan that drinks memories rather than water. Its five
> Great Roots are choked, so the tree has forgotten the legend it was planted
> to hold — the story of **Savitri and Satyavan**. A small girl frees the roots
> one by one, and with each one the tree remembers another piece of the story.
> Warm, gentle, sad-but-hopeful. Nothing frightening.

---

# THE MURALS

Six panels, one per beat of the legend. In the game each is carved into a young
banyan that rises when a root is freed, and the player walks up to read it.

**Form.** These are *bhittichitra* — Gond wall murals, the kind painted on the
mud facade of a house for a festival. So each one is:

- a **horizontal bordered panel**, 16:9, made for a wall
- a **digna border** all the way round — a band of repeating geometric pattern
  (crescents, triangles, dots), the way a Gond floor or wall is edged before it
  is filled
- figures standing on **one ground line**, at one scale, read left to right
- a **limed mud-wall ground** — warm bone-white or pale ochre — not black, since
  this is a wall and not a canvas
- the sky filled with **birds, a sun or moon disc, and pattern**; the ground
  filled with a band of waves or seeds. No blank areas.

Output 1920×1080.

### 1. The Choice
> A Gond mural panel. On the left, **Savitri**, a young woman in a vermilion
> sari patterned with rows of white dots, a sun disc with long rays behind her
> head, holding out a garland of marigold seeds. On the right, **Satyavan**, a
> young woodcutter in leaf green filled with young-shoot motifs, an axe over his
> shoulder, young banyan leaves growing out of his shoulders. Between them the
> garland arcs, drawn as a chain of yellow and white beads. At the far right
> edge, small and half-turned away, the sage **Narada** in turmeric yellow with
> a veena, one hand raised in warning — he has just told her the young man has
> one year to live. She is choosing him anyway. Her face is calm and certain.

### 2. The Fall
> A Gond mural panel. Centre, a great banyan drawn as a mass of interlocking
> patterned branches, birds nesting in it. Beneath it **Satyavan** lies fallen,
> his body in leaf green filled with shoots, his head in the lap of **Savitri**,
> who kneels in vermilion, bending over him. His axe lies on the ground beside
> him with its blade in fish-scale pattern. The forest around them is dense with
> patterned leaves and small animals watching — a deer, two birds, a snake in
> the branches. The light is going: the sun disc at the top right is half sunk
> behind a band of crescents. Her face is grief, not panic.

### 3. The Pursuit
> A Gond mural panel. On the right, **Yama**, lord of death, dark indigo skin
> filled with dot rows, a scaled crown, a moustache, a vermilion robe patterned
> with fish scales. He rides his **vahana, a great black water buffalo** whose
> huge curved white horns sweep the width of the panel and whose body is covered
> in fish-scale pattern, with a saffron ring through its nose. In Yama's hand
> the **pasha**, a looped noose of rope, and drawn along behind it a small
> glowing patterned seed — the soul of Satyavan. On the left, **Savitri** walks
> after them, alone, her sari hem flying, absolutely steady. A road of dotted
> footprints runs between them. Above, a line of black birds flying the same way.

### 4. The Seven Steps
> A Gond mural panel. **Yama** on his buffalo turns his great head back over his
> shoulder to look at **Savitri**, who is still walking behind him and will not
> stop. Between them, along the ground line, **seven footprints** drawn as seed
> shapes pressed into the road, alternating red and brown. The panel is split by
> a band of frost — a row of white crescents across the middle — because the
> land is turning cold. His expression has changed: he is no longer dismissive,
> he is listening. She is speaking, one hand raised with a finger up, arguing.
> Birds sit on the buffalo's horns.

### 5. The Boon
> A Gond mural panel. **Yama** on his buffalo, turned fully to face **Savitri**
> now, both his hands open and generous, the noose slack and lowered. Passing
> between his hands and hers, a **stream of golden dots that grows into a small
> sun** — the boon. She stands straight, and she has just won by cleverness: she
> asked for sons by Satyavan, and he has granted it without noticing that it
> gives back the husband he is carrying away. Behind Yama, a dark gateway drawn
> as two patterned pillars and a lintel of scales. Behind Savitri, the sun disc
> rising. He looks impressed, and slightly amused at himself.

### 6. The Bloom
> A Gond mural panel, the brightest of the set. An enormous **Tree of Life**
> banyan fills the whole frame, its trunk and every branch packed with different
> infill patterns, its aerial roots coming down as patterned ribbons. The
> branches are heavy with leaves, flowers, fruit, and dozens of birds of every
> colour. At its foot, small, **Savitri and Satyavan stand together**, alive, and
> a **little girl in a shawl** stands with them looking up. Deer, fish, snakes
> and peacocks are worked into the roots and branches. A sun disc top left and a
> moon disc top right. Everything in the panel is in flower.

# THE CHARACTERS

For dialogue portraits. In the game the speaker appears at the bottom-right of
the screen while the world carries on behind, and the portrait changes with what
they are feeling.

**Rules for every character sheet:**

- **Waist-up, 3:4 portrait, character fills the frame.** Head large.
- **Flat dark ground behind them**, itself filled with a fine dot pattern —
  or a transparent background if the tool supports it cleanly.
- **The same character, the same colours, the same patterns in every
  expression.** Only face, hands and posture change. Ask for them as a
  consistent sheet, and generate all of one character's moods in one go if you
  can, so they match.
- Output 900×1200.

---

## SAVITRI

> **Base.** A young woman, the heroine. Vermilion-red sari filled with rows of
> fine white dots, a jamun-purple shoulder cloth patterned with crescents, a
> turmeric-yellow hem of fish scales. Deep black hair in one long braid over her
> right shoulder — **the braid does not end as hair, it becomes a flowering
> branch putting out green leaves**. A golden sun disc with long rays behind her
> head. Gold nose ring, three strands of beads at her throat, large almond eyes,
> a small pink bindi. Warm brown skin filled with fine dot rows.

Expressions:

1. **Resolute** — level gaze straight out, chin steady, mouth closed and calm.
   Her default. Used everywhere she is simply walking or deciding.
2. **Grieving** — head bowed and tilted, eyes lowered and half-lidded, one hand
   at her chest. For the Fall.
3. **Clever** — head slightly cocked, one eyebrow up, one finger raised, the
   faintest smile. She is out-arguing Death. For the Seven Steps.
4. **Pleading** — both palms open and lifted, brows drawn up in the middle,
   leaning forward. For the Boon.
5. **Joy** — eyes crinkled, open smile, both arms lifted, the sun behind her
   blazing wider. For the Bloom.

## SATYAVAN

> **Base.** A young woodcutter, warm and easy. Bare chest in warm brown skin
> filled with young-shoot motifs, a leaf-green dhoti of comb lines, a saffron
> sash of seed shapes. Hair tied up in a knot with a green leaf through it. An
> axe with a fish-scale blade over his right shoulder. **Young banyan leaves
> grow out of his shoulders**, and a small patterned banyan tree rises directly
> behind him. Large almond eyes.

Expressions:

1. **Warm** — open, unguarded smile, looking straight out. His default.
2. **Tired** — one hand to his forehead, eyes half closed, head heavy. The
   moment before he falls.
3. **Gone** — eyes closed, face still and peaceful, the leaves on his shoulders
   drooping, the colours of his patterns gone grey-green.
4. **Waking** — eyes just opening, brows lifted in surprise, colour flooding
   back into the patterns, leaves lifting.

## YAMA — five expressions, and they are the arc of the story

> **Base.** Dharmaraja, lord of death and keeper of the accounts. Not a monster
> and not a demon: an official. Deep indigo-violet skin filled with rows of pale
> blue dots, a vermilion robe patterned with fish scales, an indigo sash of
> dots, a five-pointed crown of scales with a band of yellow beads, three
> turmeric marks on his brow, a heavy black moustache, wide white eyes. In one
> hand the **danda**, a rod topped with a golden disc; in the other the
> **pasha**, a great looped noose of saffron rope.
>
> He sits on his **vahana**, a huge black water buffalo with enormous curving
> white horns, a body of fish-scale pattern and a saffron ring through its
> nose. The buffalo fills the lower third of the frame; Yama rises from behind
> its shoulders. **He is never given the buffalo's horns himself — the buffalo
> is a separate animal.**

Expressions, in the order the player meets them:

1. **Proud** — chin lifted, looking down his nose, eyes half-lidded, the noose
   held slack and careless, body turned away as if already leaving. He has not
   decided she is worth his attention. *(First time Savitri speaks to him.)*
2. **Stern** — brows driven down, the noose raised, one palm out flat to stop
   her, the buffalo's head lowered and its horns forward. An order, not a
   threat. *(He tells her to turn back.)*
3. **Caught** — eyes wide, head turned sharply back over his shoulder, moustache
   lifted on one side, the noose gone loose in his hand. She has said something
   he did not expect and he is working out whether she is right. *(The turn of
   the argument.)*
4. **Respect** — head inclined toward her, brows level and calm, the hard line
   of the mouth softened, one palm open and turned upward, the buffalo settled
   and quiet. He has stopped being an official and started being a listener.
   *(He begins granting boons.)*
5. **Approving** — a real smile under the moustache, crinkles at the eyes, both
   hands wide open and giving, the noose coiled and put away at his belt, the
   buffalo's head up. Golden dots streaming from his palms. He has been beaten
   fairly and he is delighted about it. *(The final boon.)*

## NARADA

> **Base.** The wandering sage who carries news between the worlds. Turmeric
> yellow robe filled with seed shapes, a teal hem of crescents, white hair and a
> white beard of fine comb lines, a small topknot. A **veena** held across his
> body, its gourd a purple sphere of fish scales, its strings drawn as three
> fine white lines. Small patterned birds circle his head.

Expressions:

1. **Grave** — mouth open mid-sentence, brows up in the middle, one hand raised
   palm out. He is delivering bad news and he takes no pleasure in it.
2. **Wry** — eyes narrowed in amusement, a crooked smile, head tilted, fingers
   on the veena strings. He has seen how this ends and he is not going to say.

## THE OLD WOMAN (the Keeper)

> **Base.** The last keeper of the banyan shrine, very old. Earth-brown shawl
> filled with comb lines, drawn up over her head, a bone-white inner cloth of
> dashes, a deep red hem. Grey-white hair in a small knot. Deep lines at the
> corners of her eyes and mouth. A walking stick. Her patterns are the plainest
> of anyone's — she belongs to this valley, not to the legend.

Expressions:

1. **Weary** — eyes tired and heavy, shoulders down, both hands on the stick,
   cold. Her default at the start of the game.
2. **Hopeful** — head lifted a little, eyes brighter, one hand reaching out.
   When Savi first agrees to help.
3. **Pleased** — a small closed-mouth smile, deep crinkles, warm. After each
   root is freed.
4. **Moved** — eyes wet and wide, one hand to her mouth, mouth open slightly.
   At the bloom.
